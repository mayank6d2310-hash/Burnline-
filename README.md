# Burnline: calorie calculator and tracker

A simple fitness app that works out your daily calories and helps you track what you eat and burn.

## Features
- **Calculator:** daily calories and protein, carbs and fat targets from your age, sex, height, weight, activity level and goal (Mifflin-St Jeor formula). Metric and imperial units.
- **Food calc:** pick a food, enter the amount (grams, ml or pieces) and see its calories. Add it to your day in one tap.
- **Today:** log food and exercise and see how many calories you have left. Data is saved in your browser.

## Run it
Open `index.html` in any browser. No install needed.
